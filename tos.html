6. Candidate Experience Scores and Company Statistics

The Site automatically calculates a Candidate Experience Score for each Review. For each company, it also displays an average score, a ghosting rate, and a hire count, and the Site may rank companies on a leaderboard (together, "Scores and Statistics"). Scores and Statistics are produced by applying a fixed formula to the information reviewers choose to report. They are not our opinion, rating, or assessment of any company.

Scores and Statistics have important limits:

  • they reflect only the experiences of users who chose to submit Reviews, and those users may not be representative of all applicants;
  • a company with few Reviews may have Scores and Statistics based on very little data;
  • fields a reviewer skipped or did not remember are excluded, so an individual score may reflect only part of an application process;
  • the score bands (Poor, Fair, Good, and Excellent) and any leaderboard rankings describe where a score falls under our formula, not the overall quality of a company or its hiring practices; and
  • the ghosting rate and hire count are based only on what reviewers reported and do not represent a company's actual response rate or total number of hires.

Scores and Statistics are provided for general information only and are not statements of fact about any company. We may change the scoring method at any time. The current method is explained on our About page.
